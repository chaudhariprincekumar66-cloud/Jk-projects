Privacy Policy

How information is handled on this website.

Last reviewed: 5 November 2026

1. Information you provide

You may enter details such as your name, phone number, email address and message into forms on this website. Profile details saved through Personal Information are stored in this browser on this device. They are not synced to an account.

2. Contact and support forms

The contact and support forms currently do not send information to Shree Jageshwar or to a third-party service. Do not rely on these forms to deliver a message until a submission service is connected. You can contact the business using the phone or WhatsApp links shown on the website.

3. Local preferences

The language preference is saved in this browser so the website can remember your selection. Clearing browser storage may remove that preference and any profile details saved on this device.

4. Sharing and retention

This static website does not currently submit form details to a server. If online account, analytics, or communication services are added, this policy should be updated to explain what they collect, why they use it, and how long it is retained.

5. External links

Links to services such as WhatsApp or your phone app open outside this website. Their use is governed by the respective service provider's terms and privacy practices.
